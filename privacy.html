FileShrink Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Summary

FileShrink compresses photos, videos and PDF documents entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded. The app has no account and no advertising. The only network connection is used to process the optional FileShrink Pro purchase, as described below.

Your files

Files you choose are processed locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. FileShrink creates new, smaller copies; your originals are only deleted if you explicitly ask, and iOS asks you to confirm.

Purchases (FileShrink Pro)

FileShrink Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. To unlock it and to restore it on your devices, FileShrink uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a purchase service. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which product was bought and when, from Apple's receipt), a random anonymous app user identifier created on your device, and basic technical information such as the app version, device model, OS version, country and language. This data is used only to provide the purchase and is not linked to your name, email or files, and it is not used for tracking or advertising. Payment details are handled by Apple and are never visible to us or to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Photo library access

If you allow access, FileShrink saves compressed copies to your library and, only when you ask, deletes originals. You can grant access to selected photos only, or deny access and still compress files from the Files app.

Children

FileShrink does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
