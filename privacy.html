FileShrink Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Summary

FileShrink compresses photos, videos and PDF documents entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. The app has no account, no advertising and makes no network connections.

Your files

Files you choose are processed locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. FileShrink creates new, smaller copies; your originals are only deleted if you explicitly ask, and iOS asks you to confirm.

Photo library access

If you allow access, FileShrink reads your library to find large items and to save compressed copies. You can grant access to selected photos only, or deny access and still compress files from the Files app.

Usage statistics

An optional setting, off by default, records coarse events (for example "a video compression finished", a size range and the chosen quality) in the device's local system log. These records never leave your device and never include file names, content, locations or personal information.

Children

FileShrink does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
