Where are my compressed files?

Photos and videos are saved to your library (in the "FileShrink" album if enabled). Everything else is in the Files app under On My iPhone › FileShrink › Compressed.

Are my originals deleted?

No. FileShrink always makes a new copy. Originals are removed only if you choose to, and iOS asks you to confirm.

Why is a file not getting smaller?

Some files are already efficiently compressed. FileShrink never saves a copy that is larger than the original.

Does FileShrink upload my files?

No. All processing happens on your device. The app makes no network connections.
